## Summary counts

- **19 rules total.** **6 executable today** (31.6%) — all six are the `tier_1_prompt_independent` composition/placement rules. **13 permanently blocked** (68.4%) by a missing detector (`tier_2_content_conditional`) — 3 eye rules, 4 animal rules, 6 shape/symbol rules.
- **Zero rules are currently validated.** Every one carries `scientifically_validated: false` at the source level and an explicit non-validating literature caution at the per-rule level.
- **Threshold provenance, precisely (a finding this audit specifically looked for):**
  - **1 threshold is directly and exactly sourced**: `PSY_AR_SIZE_SMALL_016`'s 20% cutoff (the PDF literally says "20%").
  - **1 threshold has a sourced center but an invented tolerance band**: `PSY_AR_SIZE_HALF_014`'s "about 50%" → `[0.40, 0.60]`.
  - **1 threshold is a reasonable but invented numeric stand-in for a qualitative phrase**: `PSY_AR_SIZE_FULL_015`'s "covers the whole page" → `>= 0.90`.
  - **3 thresholds are entirely invented, with zero numeric anchor in the source at all**: `PSY_AR_PLACE_TOP/LEFT/RIGHT_017/018/019`'s `0.4`/`0.6` centroid-position splits — the PDF only ever says "top," "left," "right" with no boundary given.
  - **13 rules have no threshold at all** (tier 2, qualitative content-presence claims, never reach a numeric comparison because no detector exists to evaluate them).
  Per the task's own instruction ("do not invent numerical thresholds when a source only says qualitative terms... classify as qualitative/manual, threshold-unvalidated, or requiring expert annotation"): the 4 rules with a wholly- or partially-invented threshold (`014`, `015`, `017`, `018`, `019` — 5 rules, correcting the count) are correctly already tagged `IMPLEMENTED_UNVALIDATED` in the registry, which is the right category, but the registry does **not** currently distinguish "threshold sourced, validity unconfirmed" from "threshold itself invented." This document is the first place that distinction is made explicit and traceable to the exact source wording.
- **Vague/unsupported/non-operational rules**: `PSY_AR_FLOWERS_CLOUDS_SUN_010` is the one rule in the registry that is **not just unimplemented but unimplementable from a static image in principle** — its precondition ("while distracted/absent-minded," "شارد") describes the child's mental state during drawing, which no image-based extractor can ever measure. This should be flagged for possible removal or explicit reclassification as `qualitative/manual, requires informant report`, not `tier_2_content_conditional` (which implies it merely awaits a detector).
- **Duplicated/near-duplicate rule structure**: none of the 19 rules duplicate each other in content, but 4 of the 13 tier-2 rules (`004`, `005`, `006`, `007`) share an identical structural pattern (one specific animal species → one specific trait) and would all require the *same* underlying capability (a reliable animal-species classifier applied to child drawings — itself an unsolved, unvalidated computer-vision problem for this domain) — they should be planned and evaluated as one capability question, not four independent ones.

## What features (of the 59 real, already-computed objective features) are used by ANY rule today

Only 2 of the 59 real features in `features.py::objective_feature_row` are wired to a rule at all: `segmentation.bounding_box_coverage` (via `composition.bounding_box_coverage`, used by the 3 size rules) and the derived `composition.placement`/`centroid_normalized` (used by the 3 placement rules). **The other 57 real, already-computed features (colour, stroke, quality, segmentation-shape, and the shape-family entirely) are not referenced by any of the 19 rules.** This is a real, honest gap: substantial real evidence is already being computed and is currently unused by the rule layer.
